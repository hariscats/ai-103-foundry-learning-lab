Margie's Travel brochure: Tokyo Food and Design Explorer

Package summary:
The Tokyo Food and Design Explorer is a six-day itinerary for travelers interested in modern neighborhoods, design shops, culinary experiences, and efficient transit. Margie's Travel includes lodging near Shinjuku, a prepaid transit card, a guided izakaya evening, a contemporary architecture walk, and a day trip planning session.

Best fit:
This package is best for curious urban travelers, food lovers, and repeat Asia visitors who enjoy independent exploration with a few guided anchors. It is not ideal for travelers who want beaches, resorts, or a fully escorted bus tour.

Included services:
- Five nights of lodging
- Transit orientation and prepaid card
- Guided evening food tour
- Architecture and design neighborhood walk
- Optional day trip planning support for Kamakura, Nikko, or Hakone

Traveler notes:
Margie's Travel recommends packing light because hotel rooms and train station lockers may be compact. Travelers with dietary restrictions should notify Margie's Travel before booking the food tour.

Accessibility:
Tokyo transit is extensive, but some station transfers are long. Margie's Travel can help choose routes with elevator access when requested.
