Margie's Travel brochure: Paris Culture Weekend

Package summary:
The Paris Culture Weekend is a four-day city break focused on museums, food, and walkable neighborhoods. Margie's Travel includes a boutique hotel near the Latin Quarter, timed museum entry, a Seine evening cruise, and a guided market tasting tour.

Best fit:
This package is best for first-time Paris visitors, solo travelers, and couples who want a structured but flexible cultural itinerary. It is not designed for travelers who want a slow countryside stay or a full family theme-park itinerary.

Included services:
- Three nights of lodging
- Airport transfer on arrival
- Timed entry to one major museum
- Guided food market walk
- Seine cruise voucher

Traveler notes:
Margie's Travel recommends comfortable walking shoes and advance dinner reservations. Travelers should plan for stairs in older metro stations and small hotel elevators.

Accessibility:
The itinerary can be adjusted for reduced walking, but some historic areas have cobblestones and narrow sidewalks.
