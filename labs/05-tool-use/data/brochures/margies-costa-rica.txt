Margie's Travel brochure: Costa Rica Nature Reset

Package summary:
The Costa Rica Nature Reset is a seven-day guided itinerary for travelers who want wildlife, rainforest trails, and low-stress logistics. Margie's Travel includes airport transfers, eco-lodge lodging near La Fortuna, guided birding walks, a volcano viewpoint hike, and a small-group coffee farm visit.

Best fit:
This package is best for active travelers, couples, and families who enjoy nature and can handle moderate walking. It is not the best choice for travelers who want nightlife, luxury shopping, or a beach-only vacation.

Included services:
- Daily breakfast
- Three guided nature activities
- Ground transportation between San Jose, La Fortuna, and Monteverde
- One free afternoon for optional spa, hot springs, or canopy activities

Traveler notes:
Margie's Travel recommends waterproof shoes, lightweight rain gear, insect repellent, and binoculars. May through November is greener and wetter. December through April is drier and busier.

Accessibility:
Some trails include uneven surfaces and stairs. Margie's Travel can arrange lower-impact activities if requested before booking.
