Contoso Corporation - IT Support Policy (Sample)
================================================

This document contains sample IT policies used by the IT Support Agent for
grounding. It is for lab and study purposes only.

1. Password Resets
-------------------
- Employees may reset their own password through the self-service portal at
  https://passwordreset.contoso.example at any time.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and include upper case, lower case,
  a number, and a symbol.
- Passwords expire every 90 days. The system sends reminders 14, 7, and 1 day
  before expiration.
- After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes.
- If self-service reset is unavailable, contact the IT Service Desk to verify
  identity and request a manual reset. Manual resets require manager approval
  for privileged accounts.

2. Software Installation Requests
---------------------------------
- Pre-approved software is available in the Company Portal and can be installed
  without a ticket.
- For software not in the Company Portal, submit a software request ticket that
  includes the application name, business justification, and required license.
- Requests are reviewed by IT Security within 2 business days.
- Administrative rights are not granted to standard accounts; IT installs
  approved software remotely.

3. Hardware Troubleshooting
---------------------------
- For a device that will not power on, verify the power source, try a different
  cable or outlet, and perform a 30-second hard reset before opening a ticket.
- For performance issues, restart the device and confirm pending updates are
  installed. Capture the time and a description of the problem.
- Hardware under warranty (less than 3 years old) is repaired or replaced by IT.
- Peripheral replacements (keyboard, mouse, headset) are fulfilled from local
  stock within 1 business day.

4. Reporting a Lost or Stolen Device
-------------------------------------
- Report a lost or stolen device immediately to the IT Service Desk and your
  manager, and within 24 hours at the latest.
- IT will remotely lock and, if required, wipe the device to protect company
  data.
- File a police report for stolen devices and provide the report number to IT.
- A replacement device is issued after the incident is logged and approved.

5. Contacting IT Support
------------------------
- Service Desk hours: Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM local time.
- Submit tickets through the IT Support portal or email itsupport@contoso.example.
- For urgent security incidents, use the 24/7 security hotline listed on the
  intranet.
